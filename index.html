01 Project Overview

Designed for Calm Luxury.
Engineered for Operational Precision.

Bridging the gap between high-fashion editorial presentation and strict transactional e-commerce management.

Rouh Boutique was built to deliver an elevated, distraction-free shopping experience tailored to women’s modest wear (shawls, hijabs), perfumes, and accessories while providing store operators with complete control over catalog structure, inventory movements, and order fulfillment.

On the customer-facing side, the storefront departs from rigid catalog grids by introducing a magazine-cover hero layout, scheduled promotional campaigns, curated lookbooks and fixed-price product bundles, color-aware product cards, a slide-over Quick-Add confirmation drawer with related product recommendations, and multi-box gift packaging allocation at checkout.

«رُوح: تشكيلة منتقاة من الشالات والحجابات والعطور والإكسسوارات لإطلالة هادئة.»

— Official Brand Definition (src/config/brand.ts)

Under the hood, the platform is powered by Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, and PostgreSQL via Prisma 7. Every order placement executes inside a Serializable database transaction that validates live prices, evaluates coupon rules, allocates custom gift boxes, reserves variant and bundle-component stock, and dispatches interactive fulfillment notifications to a Telegram operations group.

02 Problem Analysis

The Challenge

Boutique retail in fashion and fragrances faces distinct operational bottlenecks when transitioning from manual social commerce to a scalable digital platform.

CHALLENGE 01

Traditional Shopping Workflows

Manual order collection over direct messages leads to pricing discrepancies, unverified delivery addresses across governorates, and friction when customers want to combine individual items, bundles, and gift wrapping in one order.

CHALLENGE 02

Disorganized Product Management

Fashion and fragrance items require rich relational modeling—grouping related models into product families, associating multiple hex color swatches and dedicated imagery per color, and handling custom option units or sizes per SKU.

CHALLENGE 03

Limited Online Shopping Experience

Standard e-commerce templates often feel cluttered and utilitarian, failing to reflect the calm, editorial identity of a boutique or provide ergonomic mobile browsing with instant variant selection.

CHALLENGE 04

Complex Inventory Operations

When products are sold both as standalone variants and as components inside promotional bundles, concurrent checkouts can oversell stock unless available and reserved quantities are tracked transactionally.

CHALLENGE 05

Inconsistent Product Presentation

Direct-to-bucket public uploads risk exposing cloud credentials, serving unverified file types, or leaving orphaned media records when product galleries, collection covers, and campaign banners are updated.

CHALLENGE 06

Lack of Centralized Order Management

Without an enforced order lifecycle state machine, fulfillment teams struggle to coordinate order preparation, delivery handoffs, returns, stock releases, and financial payment records in real time.

05 Systems Engineering

Technical Highlights

Key architectural decisions and production safeguards verified directly from the codebase.

Architecture 01

Feature-Sliced Domain & Request-Scoped DB Proxy

Domain rules are modularized under src/features/ (commerce, products, collections) while server-only infrastructure lives in src/lib/. Database access uses a custom JavaScript Proxy around Prisma that automatically resolves either a cached Node singleton or a per-request Worker client that disconnects cleanly via stream piping when the response finishes.

  • src/lib/db.ts — Request-scoped Prisma client proxy with SSL mode hardening.
  • src/features/commerce/pricing.ts — Pure pricing & grand-total verification functions.
Database & Concurrency 02

Serializable Inventory Transactions & Retry Engine

To prevent race conditions during concurrent checkouts, withInventoryTransaction executes order creation and stock reservations at PostgreSQL’s strictest Serializable isolation level, automatically retrying up to 3 times on P2034 serialization conflicts.

  • Atomic reservation updates checking availableQuantity - reservedQuantity >= requested.
  • Full ledger trail in StockMovement (INITIAL, RESERVATION, SALE, RELEASE, RETURN).
Media Pipeline 03

Private Object Storage & Binary Magic-Byte Verification

Media assets are stored in a private Neon S3-compatible bucket. The admin client receives a 10-minute presigned PUT URL; upon completion, the server performs a HEAD request and a partial GET (Range: bytes=0-31) to validate binary file signatures before marking the MediaAsset as AVAILABLE.

  • hasImageSignature verifies raw magic bytes for JPEG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF.
  • Storefront reads stream through /api/media/[id] without exposing bucket credentials.
Security & Auth 04

Fail-Closed Distributed Rate Limiting & RBAC

Instead of volatile in-memory counters that reset across serverless instances, consumeDistributedRateLimit uses an atomic PostgreSQL INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE time-bucket query and fails closed if storage is unreachable.

  • 5-tier Admin RBAC (OWNER, MANAGER, CATALOG, FULFILLMENT, VIEWER) + AuditLog.
  • scrypt password hashing with timingSafeEqual and same-origin request guards.
Operations & Fulfillment 05

Mobile-First Admin Workspace & Telegram Bot Sync

The admin panel (/admin) is engineered as an isolated mobile-first workspace featuring bottom-sheet editors, draft-restoration banners, preparation checklists, and IQD currency inputs. Every order status transition syncs bidirectionally with Telegram forum threads.

  • Interactive Telegram inline keyboards transition orders (PREPARING, READY, SHIPPED, COMPLETED).
  • Automatic stock deduction or reservation release upon order completion, cancellation, or return.
UI & Validation 06

Dual-Shell Responsive UI & End-to-End Zod Validation

To deliver a native-feeling experience on both phones and large monitors without Next.js hydration mismatches, ResponsiveView renders dedicated mobile and desktop shells toggled via pure CSS breakpoints, backed by strict server-side Zod validation.

  • matchesDisplayedOrderTotal prevents checkout if prices or coupons changed mid-session.
  • Strict gift-box item allocation checks ensuring bundle vs. individual item rules are respected.

06 Platform Capabilities

E-Commerce Features

A complete suite of storefront and back-office capabilities implemented across the platform.

Editorial Product Browsing

Magazine-style landing cover, campaign carousels, category filtering, search, and a mobile catalog switcher between 2-column grid and editorial full-bleed cards.

Categories & Slug Redirects

Drag-and-drop category ordering, visibility toggles, custom Lucide icon assignment, and automatic CategoryRedirect mapping when category slugs are renamed.

Product Families & Variants

Groups related products via ProductFamily reference codes, with multi-hex color swatches, custom option units (e.g., volume or size), and SKU-level stock and pricing.

Curated & Bundle Collections

Supports both CURATED lookbook collections and fixed-price BUNDLE boxes that automatically reserve inventory across all underlying component variants.

Shopping Cart & Quick-Add

Interactive Quick-Add modal for instant variant selection, slide-over cart confirmation panel with cross-sell recommendations, and persistent Wishlist management.

Gift Packaging & Checkout

Lets customers allocate specific cart items into one or more custom GiftPackagingOption boxes (enforcing min/max item limits and personal notes) with governorate-based delivery pricing.

Inventory & Low-Stock Alerts

Quick stock adjustment controls in the admin panel, per-variant low-stock thresholds, real-time reservation tracking, and automated Telegram low-stock notifications.

Discount & Coupon Engine

Supports percentage and fixed IQD discounts scoped to specific products, categories, or collections, with coupon codes, date windows, minimum order totals, and usage caps.

Admin & Financial Ledger

Full back-office suite covering order preparation checklists, customer accounts, payment collection panels, and a FinancialTransaction ledger for payments, refunds, and expenses.
